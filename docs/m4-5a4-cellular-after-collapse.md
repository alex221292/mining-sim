# M4.5a.4 — cellular updates after rock collapse

## Reproduction and cause

In the ordinary BEDROCK_ENABLED sandbox, after three detached rocks settle, successive SAND strokes could appear frozen despite the outer fixed-tick loop continuing. The headless regression uses the real brush command queue, coupled physics, occupancy, displacement and cellular simulation. In the baseline `ef72012`, a stroke over the sleeping three-body stack first falls; then a tick fails with `SWEEP: Intermediate swept material obstruction at 20,33 (SAND) for body 3`. Three identical rejections make the session `blocked`. A second stroke in free air is still painted by the command queue, but cellular simulation is not run and its top cell stays SAND. `SequentialSandAfterCollapseTest.laterSandDoesNotHangWhenSimulationTicksContinue` fails on baseline with that exact state and passes after the change. The sandbox test also draws connected STONE, cuts three supports, waits for automatic detachment and sleep, and draws three strokes.

The root cause is not an ACTIVE_REGIONS reactivation failure: InteractiveSession uses FULL_SCAN. Every successful cellular move increments the general World mutation revision. RigidBodySystem used that revision as a proxy for static terrain changes, waking all sleeping bodies on every granular/liquid move. The resulting unneeded rigid-body motion could intersect a transient swept sand cell outside final occupancy. Sweep correctly rejects that tick to preserve matter and rollback; repeated rejection blocks the session, so subsequent cellular steps are skipped. The main TPS counter measures attempts, not successful coupled/cellular ticks; F3 reports BLOCKED and the coupling failure.

## Fix and invariants

World now also publishes a monotonic rigid-terrain revision for changes involving STONE, WOOD or BEDROCK, including batched extraction and `clear`. The coupled `DISPLACE_GRANULAR` static query observes that revision; LEGACY static queries continue observing the general revision, since granular cells are solid to that policy. World mutation revision, activity notifications and the sole RockConnectivity change sink are unchanged. A NO_OP or rejected mutation does not advance the revision. General and terrain revision increments are preflighted before publication. Actual rigid terrain edits still wake sleepers; granular and liquid changes alone do not. No collision geometry, SAT, gravity, friction, sleeping thresholds, ownership or atomic failure behavior changed.

On success, occupancy and displacement still publish before the cellular phase; each attempted tick still rejects unresolved sweeps and rolls back physics. This fix prevents the demonstrated unnecessary wake, not every possible coupling rejection. A previously blocked running session still requires F6 retry or a fresh session. Sleeping rocks do not suppress the cellular step. Painting over existing matter retains the existing brush replacement semantics; tests count conserved matter where strokes are placed in empty air.

## Verification and performance

`gradlew.bat test build` covers the reproduced failure, three sequential strokes over and away from the stack, SAND/WATER/GRAVEL/SOIL conservation and occupancy checks on every tick, static terrain revisions and batches, wake and reset, F6-equivalent retry after injected failure, automatic detachment, replay and rollback. FULL_SCAN and experimental ACTIVE_REGIONS both reactivate repeated strokes; their free-fall replay matches for the tested seed and scenario. ACTIVE_REGIONS is still opt-in and is not generally guaranteed to consume randomness identically to FULL_SCAN (`m2-activity.md`).

`gradlew.bat largeCompoundBenchmark --args="large large"`: 64×64 coupled 134/134/526-cell fixture, 60 Hz simulation, OpenJDK 25.0.5, Gradle 9.2.1, second run warm, identical settings. Baseline vs fixed mean/p95 full tick: settling ticks 30–89 **13.200/13.625 vs 13.382/13.763 ms**; sleeping ticks 240–359 **0.671/0.706 vs 0.685/0.740 ms**. Both first sleep at tick 229. This benchmark contains no painted sand and measures preservation of large-body performance, not GUI FPS. Measurements vary by machine and JVM.

Manual Swing verification remains outstanding: start `gradlew.bat run`, draw a BEDROCK-attached STONE structure, cut several sections, let them sleep, then draw three separated SAND strokes above the pile and in free air; repeat with WATER, GRAVEL and SOIL. Use F3 to check cellular progress and failures; try removing a support, F6 after a rejection, and C reset. These steps were not performed in a visible GUI.
