# M4.4b.2a — static contact detection

`StaticCollisionQuery` is an explicitly called, read-only discrete query, independent of the simulation loop. It does not change the world, fragment or body. Positions and depths are in world-cell units (one cell is 0.1 m); Y increases downward. The normal points from the body toward the static cell or outward through the boundary. A zero-depth touch is not reported. STONE, BEDROCK, WOOD, SAND, SOIL and GRAVEL are obstacles; AIR and WATER are not.

The broad phase enumerates the current rotated AABB clipped to the world, in row-major order. The narrow phase applies square SAT to each actual occupied fragment cell; concave gaps remain empty. For overlapping squares the intersection polygon is clipped against the four edges of the static square. The arithmetic mean of its vertices is a deterministic representative point inside the overlap, not necessarily a point on the original unpenetrated surface or a time-of-impact point. Normals and depths come from the minimum-overlap SAT axis. Identical-center ties use axis order. Non-finite contact calculations are rejected.

All four boundaries are distinct half-planes bounding `[0,width] × [0,height]`. Penetrating transformed cell vertices produce points projected to the corresponding boundary and an outward normal. Where a fragment cell already overlaps a bottom-row BEDROCK cell, bottom-boundary vertices within that cell's horizontal span are suppressed; other boundary contacts remain. This is a discrete overlap convention, not recovery from arbitrary deep penetration.

Contacts with nearly identical normals on adjacent, coplanar world cells form connected clusters. Boundary contacts cluster only with the same boundary and adjacent fragment cells. Each cluster retains at most two contacts, at tangent extrema, in deterministic order. Different normals and disconnected surfaces remain separate. This is an approximation: the representative point and retained depth are not a complete contact manifold, especially at deep penetration or complicated seams.

Candidate cells and narrow-phase checks have caller-supplied positive limits; exceeding either throws `IllegalStateException` rather than returning partial results. Reduction is additionally capped at 1,000 raw contacts. Bounds checking and the limit apply even when no contact is returned. The query is deterministic for the same inputs and runtime floating-point behavior.

There is **no response**, impulse, friction, restitution, position correction, sweep, tunnelling prevention, body-body collision, sleeping, occupancy or Swing integration. Free-motion bodies may pass through obstacles between queries. A future solver must explicitly handle the approximation and work-limit failures; these results alone do not make gameplay collision-safe.
